Our Story

Bridging the diaspora and Kenyan law

Diaspora Legal Support Services (DLSS) was founded in 2026 to close a persistent gap: people living abroad who need reliable access to Kenya’s legal system, but face distance, unfamiliar processes, and limited trusted guidance.

From property and succession to business, family matters, and documentation, we help clients work with verified advocates who understand both diaspora realities and Kenyan jurisdiction — so legal support feels clear, confidential, and within reach, wherever you are.
